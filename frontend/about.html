About Prompt Universe

Prompt Universe is a live, visual map of the artificial intelligence landscape — tracking the relative popularity of the world's most-used AI tools and refreshing every single day.

What is this?

The AI ecosystem moves faster than any other corner of technology. New tools launch weekly, yesterday's leader becomes today's afterthought, and it's genuinely hard to keep track of what people are actually using. Prompt Universe answers one simple question at a glance: which AI tools matter right now?

Every tool is shown as a glowing bubble. The bigger the bubble, the more popular the tool. Bubbles are arranged in a dense cluster over a world map, connected like a living neural network. Hover or tap any bubble to see details — what the tool does, when it launched, its current popularity score and its week-over-week trend.

How popularity is measured

Each tool is assigned a popularity score from 1 to 100. This score is a relative measure — ChatGPT, as the most widely used AI tool globally, anchors the top of the scale. Every other tool is scored relative to that benchmark.

The scores are generated daily by analysing signals that reflect genuine real-world adoption:

  • Search trends — how often people search for each tool
  • Web traffic — relative visitor volume to each tool's website
  • Active usage — monthly active users and app downloads where available
  • Developer adoption — momentum within the developer community
  • Social signals — mentions and discussion across the web

The trend value shown for each tool indicates its week-over-week movement — a positive number means the tool is rising in popularity, a negative number means it's cooling off.

How often is it updated?

The entire dataset refreshes automatically once every 24 hours. Each morning the rankings, scores and trends reflect the most current data available — so it's worth checking back daily to see who's climbing and who's falling.

Frequently asked questions

Is Prompt Universe affiliated with any of these AI companies?

No. Prompt Universe is an independent project. We don't represent, endorse or receive payment from any of the tools shown. The rankings are purely a reflection of measured popularity.

Why is my favourite tool not on the map?

The map focuses on the most popular tools by measurable usage. Newer or more niche tools may not yet have enough signal to rank. As they grow, they'll appear automatically.

Can I use this data on my own site?

Yes — there's an embeddable version available. Click the "Embed" button on the main page to get a snippet you can paste into any website, blog or newsletter.

Can I click through to a tool?

Absolutely. Clicking any bubble opens that tool's official website in a new tab, so you can try it for yourself.
